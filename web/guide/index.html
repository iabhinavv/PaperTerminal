Self-hosted paper trading terminal

Trade ten markets without losing anything

PaperTerminal looks like a Bloomberg screen because that is the vocabulary the industry actually uses. It exists to teach the two things trading simulators almost always leave out: what leverage costs you while you hold it, and what tax takes once you finally sell.

Start with three commands

Type AAPL and press GO to load a security. Type PORT to see what you own. Type MARG to see what it is costing you. Everything else follows from those three.

You start small on purpose

$1,000, plus $1,000 every Monday. Small enough that leverage is tempting, which is precisely the situation the terminal is designed to teach you about. The pattern day trader rule will catch you. So will interest.

Nothing here is advice

Derivatives are model-priced rather than quoted, the tax engine is a simplified model of US federal rules, and no part of this is a recommendation. It is a teaching tool. Talk to a professional before risking real money.
